Round 5: rear as one convex shape, glass that sits on the body (rc=shape)

The greenhouse now starts at the belt at the haunch's width and tumbles home, so the outline from roof to haunch is one convex curve; door and window grids are denser near the roof edge so nothing floats. Opt-in; default asset unchanged. Tunable live: index.html?rc=shape&sh=w:0.8;tb:0.16.

Recognition first

The cues people use to recognise the car were measured from the reference orthographics (recog/spec.js) and only scaled: the long shallow screen and roof peak behind the wheelbase centre, the six-light greenhouse, slatted bar over a chrome-framed honeycomb grille, swept projector headlamps with an amber indicator, wide fog recesses, wrap tail lamps, five-spoke alloys. Cuteness comes from proportion (bigger wheels, shorter wheelbase, softer volumes), not new shapes.

Triangle optimisation

One LOD table drives every primitive; each tier keeps the same parts, pivots and cues and drops only what its camera distance cannot resolve. Panels are dense enough to dent (an enforced budget), which is why LOD1 stops near 4.6 k.

Smash sequence

Dents accumulate, doors and bonnet hang on hinges, then fly off; a wheel breaks free and rolls; glass shatters. Detached parts keep their dents and momentum (Rapier hull colliders).

Contract and performance gates

Rebuilt in JS for code-defined assets: node tools/check_asset.mjs --bake --selftest (about 35 s). The original Blender-track validator, run on the same GLBs, agrees on every structural rule and fails only the four the procedural profile replaces on purpose (UV atlas ×2, morph targets, paint-mask texture).
