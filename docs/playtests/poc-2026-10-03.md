# POC round 1: owner feedback (2026-10-03)

Source: the owner's review of `https://jammers-preview.dilger.dev/poc/` (TV mocks, phone mocks, engine synth, style
frames, brand sheet) plus four reference screens, saved in `art/ui/refs/owner-2026-10-03/`. The owner will send more;
later feedback becomes round 2 in `poc-<date>.md`. Rulings are in `docs/policies/owner-direction-2026-09-29.md`
(R95–R103). Each item below says which bead carries it. **Round status: open** (P1-U04 closes only on the owner's verdict).

## Reference screens (what to take from them)

`art/ui/refs/owner-2026-10-03/`: `tv-pause-menu.jpg`, `tv-intermission-highlights.jpg`, `phone-intermission-vote.jpg`,
`phone-join.jpg`. **Take the UI elements and design language, not the content** (not the copy, vote cards, car art or mode
names). The owner likes:

1. A non-web feel: appealing elements that are *not* strictly grid-aligned. The game UI is Rust/WASM, so web layout
   constraints don't apply.
2. Big images and renders.
3. A contrasting backing banner behind heading type.
4. Still very compact.
5. Slants, high contrast and readability that feel alive; ours was sterile with rounded corners on every element.
6. Colour behind some text.

## Decisions

### Audio (`P1-A04c`)
- **POC1-01** The Cruz Missile has **no turbo** in its engine sound. Boost/turbo is profile data, so it's off in
  `cruz-missile.json` and a per-vehicle feature (A04b's sound lab exposes it). → R103.
- **POC1-02** Add engine **start and stop** sounds and a start/stop sim to the synth (ignition crank and catch, shut-down
  spool-down), playable from the synth page and exposed to in-game audio (A05). → R103.

### Design language (`P1-U01.3`)
- **POC1-03** Move the guide, tokens and component sheet toward the reference screens' language (items 1–6 above):
  slants, banner-backed headings, colour behind text, hand-made non-grid placement, big renders, no uniform rounded
  corners. Take it as a language shift, not a copy. → R102. Blocks U02.3, U02.4, U03.2.

### TV: grid and tiles (`P1-U02.2`)
- **POC1-04** **Every player tile has the same area.** Broken at N = 5, 10 and around 26–27 (some tiles larger); 24 is
  right. This supersedes §3a's "last row centred, tiles grow wider". Leftover cells hold the join QR, standings or
  captions. → R95 (carries to R04).
- **POC1-05** Keep the join/QR shown to the sides as in the mock (liked).
- **POC1-06** At 32 tiles the layout is wrong again, and the HUD is too large: the place and lap indicators must be
  smaller. Every state's pill is **one line high** and scales down with the screen.
- **POC1-07** The "That's you" label says **"Cooee #N"** (the player's number) and sits over a **transparent colour flash
  with high exposure, tweened**, behind it. → R99.
- **POC1-14** Name plates are only for Derby or modes sharing one screen (Overview). → R100.

### TV: footer, host menu, pause (`P1-U02.3`)
- **POC1-08** A **footer toolbar** carries pause and the host's controls, game-specific info (player count, stats,
  readouts), the logo, and the **room code with the domain**. The QR goes around the player screens if it fits, otherwise
  in the footer; **hovering the QR enlarges it for a while and pauses the game**. → R96, R97.
- **POC1-09** The **host puck moves into the footer menu** and is expanded (look at Physical Soccer for ideas). **Pause is
  a top-level button.** Any submenu that needs the full screen **pauses first**.
- **POC1-10** **No diagnostics over player screens.** The footer can expand to show them while playing. Settings, End
  round and Disband all live in the pause flow, restyled like `tv-pause-menu.jpg` (style: POC1-03).
- **POC1-11** **Captions are global**, not on one player's screen: in the footer or a spare cell. Host settings choose
  whether the player list, QR and captions are **dynamic (reflowing between footer and spare cells) or static**, so it can
  be tuned. → R96.

### TV: cameras and countdown (`P1-U05.2`)
- **POC1-12** Cameras sit **higher and show the track ahead**; too much of the tile is useless scene. First person is
  **segmented** so the screen maximises what helps the driver. **Cars are noticeably smaller by default**, with a
  **setting for camera distance** because playtests may want different values at high vs low player counts, or per player
  preference. → R98 (carries to R05; the mocks' FP/TP framing feeds its TUNE values).
- **POC1-13** The 3-2-1 countdown is **one full-screen overlay with the same high-exposure flash**, not one per tile. → R99.

### TV: lobby, intermission, end of round (`P1-U02.4`)
- **POC1-15** Rethink the **lobby / warm-up**: the cars can't be seen now. Perhaps generic footage; decide how a warm-up
  would work. Apply POC1-03.
- **POC1-16** Intermission with round highlights is the right direction, but there's wasted space beside the QR.
  **Maximise the highlights**; more content on the sides of the main replay; the QR stays prominent.
- **POC1-17** The end-of-round screen is fine; apply the new style (POC1-03).

### Phone controller (`P1-U03.2`)
- **POC1-18** **Boost and power-ups move to the centre top**, between both sides.
- **POC1-19** The **lobby screen needs the most work**: it must support **choosing among multiple vehicles**.
- **POC1-20** The **tutorial is a bit bigger and more central**, and **moving the sticks (etc.) advances it** when done
  successfully.
- **POC1-21** The controller **always shows the player's identify colour**, and plays the **same timed high-exposure flash**
  as the TV. → R99.
- **POC1-22** The phone is **held in landscape**: encourage it, and encourage **tap for fullscreen and a wake lock**. →
  R101.
