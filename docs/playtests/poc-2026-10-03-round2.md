## Decisions

### In-world graphics (`P1-U05.3`)
- **POC2-01** Corner chevrons: a **row of posts each with one chevron** (the common road style). → R104.
- **POC2-02** Replace the tyre walls and rounded-tyre rails with **Australian steel guard rail** "at some point". The owner
  recalls a reference image: it is `art/references/australia/generated/biome-outback-dirt-and-bitumen.png` (W-beam on
  square posts along the bitumen, flared end terminal at the dirt, yellow delineators). → R105.
- **POC2-03** Drop the **"Checkpoint N"** gantries. → R106. Open question for the owner, not decided here: do lap-validity
  checkpoints remain as invisible gameplay (plan §8)? Default: yes, invisible, until told otherwise.
- **POC2-04** If a graphic goes over points, **and** to revamp the finish line, use the gantry reference's style plus the
  earlier style points (R102: slants, contrast banner, big type). → R106.
- **POC2-05** Paint colours look good: keep.

### Derby Overview (`P1-U05.4`)
- **POC2-06** The camera movement **makes the owner motion sick**: much smoother. → R107.
- **POC2-07** **Predictive scaling** so drivers can see where they are going and adjust. → R107.
- **POC2-08** Near-fixed camera, derby designed to suit it, **generative elements (Olgas etc.) visible from it**, more later.

### Look: shaders, lighting, engine features (`P1-U05.5`)
- **POC2-09** Overall tone: **Mad Max**. Add several shader directions to the POC. → R108.
- **POC2-10** Ink outline: **undecided**; if kept, **more around the outside of the car** than now.
- **POC2-11** Lighting can improve; consider the best the engine offers and advanced techniques; **push visuals within
  budgets**.
- **POC2-12** **Anisotropic filtering** (or similar) needed: some lines alias/shimmer.
- **POC2-13** The textured (halftone) **shadow concept is liked, but it renders on top of the car**: fix the layering.
- **POC2-14** Emissive effects are OK for now; they need to be **better and more varied** later.

### Motion reel (`P1-U05.6`)
- **POC2-15** **Full vs reduced motion is indistinguishable** in the autoplay: make the difference visible.

## Barrier spec (R105)
Barrier spec (describe, don't depend on the image): galvanised steel W-beam rail, a corrugated 'W' profile in a continuous run, bolted to square posts at regular spacing, mounted at roughly knee-to-hip height.
