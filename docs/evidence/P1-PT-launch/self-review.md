## Defects found and fixed

- The old physical receiving table ended before the design flight; extend it and blend the descent to the road within the existing curvature limit. Measure uprightness through touchdown and exit.
- Renderer geometry omitted the new descent transition; update its profile/subdivision and compare actual uploaded f32 triangle bytes with collision geometry.
- Initial near-camera captures crowded the car against the bottom edge. Recapture at mid distance and full resolution. WebKit's real welcome caption initially obscured the rear tyres; wait for the caption to finish naturally, without hiding UI.
