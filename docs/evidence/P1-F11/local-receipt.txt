## Recipe corrections made while building the page (the adapter's first draft was wrong here)

 1. r182 has PostProcessing, not RenderPipeline.
 2. MeshToonNodeMaterial dots the object-space normal attribute with a view-space light: replaced by an own LightingModel.
 3. LightingModel.direct() lightColor keeps ~half the sun inside cast shadows (luminance 1.0 vs 3.0 lit, sun 2.05): now baseColorNode x shadowNode.
 4. NodeMaterial multiplies instanceColor into the whole colour: paint key moved to an instanced attribute.
 5. Linear-depth Laplacian drew a band along the horizon at small tile sizes: switched to the device-depth Laplacian x z / depthScale.
 6. Linear filtering of id/normal/depth drew a phantom line on the far plane: Nearest.
 7. Sky is cleared to (0,0,0,1) in the extra MRT targets (shade reads 1.0): sky classified by depth only.
 8. atan2 is "overloaded" in TSL; use atan(y, x).
 9. GLSL: `patch` is a reserved word in GLSL ES 3.0.
