# P1-F11 acceptance item 3: fresh-agent test of `jammers-look`

Task: following only the skill, render the Cruz Missile on red dirt under a sun with a toon ramp, ink outlines and halftone
shadows. Result: all three treatments rendered on the first run of the TSL / WebGPURenderer route; the rest of the time went on
framing and lighting, not on the skill's recipes.

Where each treatment is visible:

- **Toon ramp (3 tones)**: `hero.png` (lit bonnet and roof, mid-tone nose facets and windscreen frame, shaded flank) and `side.png`
  (lit roof panel and bonnet, mid-tone A-pillar and bonnet slope, shaded flank). The ramp is clearest as three levels in
  `hero-dbg-shade.png` (black = lit, grey = mid, white = shadow). It reads less strongly than the other two treatments: the
  difference between the lit and mid tones is subtle in the beauty pass, and a flank facing away from the sun is always the
  darkest tone plus dots. I did not retune `tones` / `cuts`.
- **Ink outlines**: `hero.png`, `side.png` (navy `#15203A` silhouette and panel-crease lines, about 3 px, around the car and
  the wheels). The `hero-dbg-edge.png` channel shows the outline pass in isolation (silhouette, creases, door seams, wheels).
- **Halftone in shadows**: `crop-halftone-2x.png` (best), `hero.png`, `side.png`. Dots at 45 degrees, cell 8 px, on the cast
  ground shadow and on the car's shaded flank; lit areas and emissive pixels (headlights, LED bar, tail lamp) are clean.

I tried three other hero variants (sun azimuth 15 with the camera front-right and rear-right, and azimuth -70 with a low sun and the camera front-left; renders not kept) to find a view where the
shadow is visible and not hidden behind the car. Rear-right with `sunAz 15` showed all three treatments best, but a front view
is the better hero for this car, so I kept `sunAz -42` and moved the camera closer.

## Gaps in the skill (what I had to guess or look up)

1. **Import map is only described in words** (SKILL.md "Setup facts": "import map points `three` **and** `three/webgpu` at the same
   `three.webgpu.js`"). A working page also needs `three/tsl` -> `build/three.tsl.js` and `three/addons/` -> `examples/jsm/`
   (`BloomNode.js` imports `three/webgpu` and `three/tsl`). I read the `exports` map in `node_modules/three/package.json`. Put the
   four-line map in SKILL.md or recipes.md.
2. **No import header for the recipe blocks** (recipes.md, every TSL block). The blocks use about 45 TSL names (`property`,
   `BRDF_Lambert`, `diffuseColor`, `perspectiveDepthToViewZ`, `screenSize`, `screenCoordinate`, `directionToColor`, ...) and
   `THREE.LightingModel`, `THREE.MeshToonNodeMaterial`, `THREE.PostProcessing`. I guessed the list and verified each name against
   `three.tsl.js` / `three.webgpu.js` with `check-names.mjs` (all exist). `bloom` and `fxaa` are not in `three/tsl`; they come from
   `three/addons/tsl/display/BloomNode.js` and `FXAANode.js`, which only the vendored `post-processing.md` shows. recipes.md
   should list the exact imports once.
3. **`renderer.shadowMap.enabled = true` is never stated.** SKILL.md "Setup facts" lists `await renderer.init()`, tone mapping and
   flat albedo; the `rig` block sets `sun.castShadow` and the shadow camera but not the renderer flag. Also unstated: the ground must
   have `receiveShadow = true` (the `wiring` block sets it only on the car instances, and does not create the ground at all).
4. **`main.js#wiring` is not self-contained.** It uses `groups`, `poses`, `PAINTS`, `STATE`, `nCars`, `view`, `ground`,
   `RED_EARTH`, `makeAtlas` without saying where they come from. The real trap: `meshes[0].geometry.clone()` reuses the first
   mesh's geometry for the whole group, which is right for the four wheels (the `L` ones are mirrored by a Y rotation) but wrong for
   core / front / back / the four doors, whose geometries differ. Spike J's `build()` returns one `Mesh` per part with
   `userData.rest`, so I grouped by part name (all four wheels together under `wheel`). The skill should say how to build
   `groups` from `build()` output, and that `RED_EARTH` is `#C8622E` (it is in the SKILL.md header, but not tied to the variable).
5. **Orientation conventions are not stated.** The model faces +Z and its right side is +X (Spike J header); `rig` puts the sun at
   `(sin(az)*9.5, 9, cos(az)*9.5)`, so `sunAz -42` is front-left of the car. With a fixed 43 degree sun the shadow falls
   away from the sun, so a front three-quarter hero hides the shadow unless the camera is on the side opposite the sun. I needed
   three test renders to find a view. One line in SKILL.md ("for a hero where the cast shadow shows, put the camera on the side opposite the
   sun azimuth") would save that.
6. **Halftone strength by shade level is not documented.** The `post` comment says "dot radius grows with shade, 0.42 = about 55%
   coverage at full shade", but not what `jjShade` values the ramp produces. From the recipe tones, `jjShade = 1 - tone*lit`: lit 0, mid 0.28, form-shadow 0.6 (computed dot radius about 0.1 cell, tiny) and
   cast-shadowed 1.0 (full dots); `hero-dbg-shade.png` shows the three levels. On the car, faces turned away from the sun appear to be
   self-shadowed by the shadow map (the flank shows full-size dots), not the small form-shadow dots. Good to know before
   tuning `cuts` or `tones`.
7. **`frame` block uses undefined `q`, `H`, `draw`.** `H` is the tile's pixel height, so `renderer.setPixelRatio(1)` is
   needed for `tierFor(H)` to be right. Not said.
8. **Playwright default-flag claim is untested here.** SKILL.md says plain `chromium.launch({ channel: 'chromium' })` gets WebGPU
   on this Mac; I used the flags from my task (`--enable-unsafe-webgpu --use-angle=metal`) and did not test without them.
9. **Sun shadow frustum is +/-9 m around the origin** (`rig`), so ground shadows stop 9 m out. Fine for one car; the skill does not
   say how to follow a car or cover a track.
10. Minor: `updateLookCamera(look, camera)` has to be called after every fov / aspect change (I called it once, after the camera was
    placed); the `dbg=` list in the `post` comment is accurate and worked for `shade`, `edge`, `halftone`.

Nothing in the recipes was wrong: the trap list in SKILL.md (r182 `PostProcessing`, Nearest targets, `samples: 0`, sky by depth)
matched what I saw, and I hit none of those traps because the blocks already contain the fixes.
