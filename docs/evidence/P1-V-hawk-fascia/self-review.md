## Defects found and fixed

- Slab-like upper sides: increased shoulder taper and sill tuck; door seams now follow the sloped skin rather than floating outside it.
- Front intake tapered the wrong way and lamps were thin slashes: reversed intake taper, broadened and framed headlamps, shaped the corner LED slots and adjusted the bumper widths.
- Rear lacked the substantial surround: shaped six-sided exhaust recess, painted lip and dark bevel; larger flat-top hexagonal tips; boot seam; broader wing; red lamp frames with grey inset.
- Nonplanar rear-cap fan used a different triangulation from its decals: shared actual triangles, removing broken stripe fragments.
- Rear projection could pass through a quarter and hit the doors/front fender, corrupting bounds and hinges: constrained fascia projection to its owning end part. Coarse door sections retain the same hinge edge as LOD0. All 42 focused tests pass.
- Coarse tiers fragmented lamps and stripes: continuous flat fascia graphics, correct layer depth behind grilles/intakes. Final LOD1/2 front/rear captures inspected.

## Remaining defects / limitations

- Owner likeness verdict pending. Weighted silhouette IoU is .9495 / .9472 / .9362, lower than the previous pass, mainly in top view from wider ends/wing. This is shown honestly in the comparisons. LOD2 is below the method’s approximate .95 target.
