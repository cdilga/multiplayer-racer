# br-gw74.7: jumps that fly and land

## Change
- **Tabletop jumps** (`crates/jj-procgen/src/features`, generator version 8): a 7-10 m kicker ramp (height ∝ (u/l)^1.6,
  mean slope 0.16-0.21) up to a 1.1-2.1 m lip, a 4-8 m table at lip height, then a landing ramp at 0.16 back down to
  the road, 6 m wide with 1.5 m sloped sides. Too slow lands on the table; at racing speed the car lands down the
  landing ramp. Was an 8 % wedge with a 30-60 cm lip and a drop.
- **Landability rule** (`jj-map` validator, `limits::jump_flight_m`, `JUMP_DESIGN_SPEED_MPS` = 26 m/s, the autopilot's
  top speed): a car leaving the lip at the design speed must come down inside the jump's reserved landing. The
  generator sizes each landing from the same function (libm, so native and WASM bytes match).
- **Air self-levelling** (profile data `air_level_torque` 3200 N·m/rad, `air_level_damping` 2200 N·m·s; only in
  near-upright flight, tilt under 45°): with a second of air, a held throttle (the stick's air pitch) tipped cars onto
  their noses; now they land level. The tuning menu (br-2sdu.1) can change or zero both.
- Hand-made maps with the old jump (greybox, surface-strips, yard, duel-yard, broken-no-gates) get a 28 m landing (the
  greybox jump flies 26.4 m at 26 m/s); regenerated by their scripts, greybox hash re-blessed.

## Tests
- `crates/jj-procgen/tests/jumps.rs` (ten seeds, every jump, 15/22/26/30 m/s, air time counted after the spawn
  settles): every landing upright (landing up·y > 0.8, min > 0.8, end > 0.9), no recovery up to 26 m/s, every jump
  airborne ≥ 0.2 s at 22-26 m/s, mean air at racing speed **1.02 s** (the old wedges: 0.35 s,
  `docs/evidence/P1-M03d/jump-scenarios.txt`). Table: `jump-scenarios.txt`.
- `crates/jj-map/tests/v01_cases.rs` `landing_checks`: a 25 m landing behind a 1 m lip on a 6 m ramp is rejected (a car
  at 26 m/s flies about 27 m); 32 m passes.
- Scenario updates with reasons: `air-level` (no input lands level now; the baselines differ in yaw rate),
  `air-control-duel` (well-timed air control no longer gains time over a plain run that levels itself; over-rotating
  still costs 0.2 s). Procgen goldens re-blessed (version 8).
- All crate tests (234) and `jj sim` scenarios pass; clippy clean.
