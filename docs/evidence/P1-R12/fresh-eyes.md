## Coverage rule (identity and road ahead) and reduced motion

- Identity: paint colour stays readable in almost every frame. The starburst petals cover the roof and rear window of the hit car for the moment they are shown (impact-sparks-1tile, detach-damage-1tile), which is acceptable for a short burst. The damage smoke on the purple car (damage-smoke-1tile, bottom-left) and in the top-right tile of wreck-fire-4tiles hides most of the car body, and the heavy trails in wreck-fire-1tile hang over the road for several car lengths. That is the weakest area against the rule.
- Road ahead: the dust and smoke trails sit behind the followed car and mostly stay off the road ahead. In wreck-fire-4tiles the dark smoke covers the track view in the upper half of the tiles. The road ahead is still visible in every frame.
- Reduced motion: impact-sparks-1tile-reduced removes the large starburst and keeps a few small sparks, so flashes are clearly toned down. Dust and smoke are left as they were, which seems appropriate.
- Look: effects are flat translucent discs. Dust reads warm and fits the palette. The smoke discs have no ink outline and no halftone, so they look less comic than the inked cars, clouds and starbursts, and they are less Mad Max than the references. The starbursts are the only clearly inked effect.

## Defects

1. detach-damage-1tile, bottom-left, and landing-1tile, bottom edge: dust discs are cut off with a hard flat horizontal edge at the bottom of the frame (a clipped sphere), which breaks the soft look. Minor.
2. wreck-fire-overview.jpg, centre: from the top-down view the wreck shows only a small glow blob with no smoke or fire shape; the family is hard to read from overview range. Minor, since the overview is not a tile count.
3. damage-smoke-1tile.jpg, lower left: the damage smoke on the purple car is big and dark and hides the car's rear and part of its paint. wreck-fire-4tiles.jpg, top-right tile, shows the same. This runs against "never cover car identity". Minor, because the car is damaged and the cloud is brief.
4. landing-1tile.jpg, landing-4tiles.jpg, landing-24tiles.jpg: landing puffs are plain round dust discs, hard to tell from driving dust, and there is no ring or distinct burst. Minor.
5. gravel-spray-1tile.jpg and all 24-tile frames: gravel pebbles are tiny grey dots and are almost invisible at 24 tiles. Minor.
6. Smoke and dust discs have no ink outline or halftone, unlike the cars, clouds and starburst bursts, so they look out of style with the accepted comic look. Minor.
7. Brake lamps: no frame shows a clear brake-lamp state compared with tail lamps, so that part of the lamp acceptance is not demonstrated. Minor.
8. wreck-fire-1tile.jpg: the wreck fire glow is cut off at the right edge, so it is only partly framed at 1 tile (it is fully visible in impact-sparks-1tile and hits-4tiles). Minor.

No blocking defects found.

## Verdict: PASS

Every effect family is readable somewhere at 1, 4 and 24 tiles. The short bursts (hits, landings, detach) are held in their own frames, at all three tile counts, and the starbursts, loose panels, blue boost flames, tyre smoke, red dirt dust, damage smoke and wreck fire with glow are all clearly visible. The lamps are emissive and read well. Reduced motion visibly tones the impact flash down. The effects mostly stay off the car's paint and off the road ahead, with the exception of the damage smoke clouds, which hide a car body for a while. The remaining defects are minor: the clipped dust edge, the landing puff that looks like normal dust, the weak gravel at 24 tiles, the missing ink outline on smoke and dust, and the overview of the wreck. None of these blocks acceptance.
