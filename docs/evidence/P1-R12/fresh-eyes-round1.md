## Coverage rule (identity and road ahead) and reduced motion

- Identity: paint colour is never hidden. Discs sit under and behind the cars, with a slight overlap on the bumper of the followed car in dust-dirt-1tile and tyre-smoke-1tile. The followed car's paint remains clear.
- Road ahead: not covered in any frame. The effects are behind or beside the cars.
- Reduced motion: the reduced capture is not visibly different from the normal one. Nothing in these images shows that flashes are toned down. This cannot be judged from the evidence, because the normal frame contains no strong flash.
- Look: smoke and dust are flat, un-outlined, uniformly translucent circles with a soft edge. Cars, clouds and barriers have ink outlines, so the particles look stuck on rather than part of the comic style. The reference's grit and tracks are missing.

## Defects

1. wreck-fire-overview.jpg, whole frame: no fire and no usable glow on any wreck. Only one tiny yellow blob. Fails "fire and a glow on wrecks".
2. detach-damage-1tile.jpg, behind the purple car: no visible burst on detach, and no smoke from the damaged car. Only a faint tan puff and a ground panel.
3. impact-sparks-1tile.jpg and impact-sparks-1tile-reduced.jpg: no impact flash and no convincing sparks. Both frames show mostly dust discs. Reduced motion cannot be checked.
4. gravel-spray-1tile.jpg: the frame shows boost, not gravel. No gravel spray is visible at the followed car.
5. dust-dirt-1tile.jpg, landing-1tile.jpg, tyre-smoke-1tile.jpg: particles are flat, hard-edged translucent discs with no ink outline or toon shading. They look out of place in the Fury road comic look. Dust on dirt is tan, not red.
6. boost-blue-1tile.jpg, mixed-24tiles.jpg: the "flame" is two round pale-blue discs with no flame shape or core and no blue-at-full intensity cue.
7. mixed-24tiles.jpg: at 24 tiles dust, smoke and sparks are dots of a few pixels. Only boost reads. Damage, fire, impact and detach effects are absent from the 24-tile shot.
8. mixed-4tiles.jpg: only tyre smoke and small dust appear. No boost, sparks, damage or fire at 4 tiles, so the "every family at 1, 4 and 24" requirement is not met.
9. All frames: brake lamps cannot be told apart from tail lamps.
10. dust-dirt-1tile.jpg bottom, tyre-smoke-1tile.jpg bottom: the big discs overlap the lower rear of the followed car. Mild, but it is the closest the effects come to violating the coverage rule.

## Verdict: FAIL

The evidence does not show the full effect set. Dust, tyre smoke, landing puffs, boost and tail-lamp glow read at 1 tile, and tyre smoke and boost are still visible at 4 and 24 tiles. Identity and road ahead are never covered. But fire and glow on wrecks, smoke from damaged cars, a clear detach burst, impact flash and convincing sparks, and gravel spray are missing or too weak to call. The 4 and 24 tile shots lack most families. The particles are flat un-outlined discs that do not match the inked comic look. The reduced-motion frame cannot be told apart from the normal one, so the tone-down is unproven. Fix the wreck fire and glow, damage smoke, impact and detach bursts, and gravel, restyle the particles, then re-capture all families at 1, 4 and 24 tiles.
