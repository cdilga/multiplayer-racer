The team

Chris Dilger owns, designs and directs Joystick Jammers. The code, the models and the tools are written by Claude (Anthropic) coding agents working to his direction, with each result checked in a running browser before it goes in.

Inspirations

Couch racers where everyone shares one screen and short, loud rounds beat long ones: Micro Machines and Mario Kart are the honest touchstones, along with the cars, roads and red dirt of the Australian outback. We took the idea, nothing else: no code, art or audio comes from those games, and none comes from any other project of ours.

The cars are bubbly caricatures made up for this game. Any real car they remind you of is a nod, not an endorsement; the makers aren't involved.

Music

        
The menu and race music is instrumental, generated for this game with YuE2-3B on the owner's own GPU machine, then edited into loops and loudness-matched. The prompts, seeds and model revision are kept with each track.

        
Licence note: the YuE2 model weights are CC BY-NC 4.0 with an extra permission for individual creators to use their outputs commercially. That fits an individual's playtest; it has to be looked at again before any company or commercial release.

      


      

        
The announcer

        
The announcer is the owner's own voice. His recordings were cloned with Qwen3-TTS (1.7B) on the owner's GPU machine; a few lines use the smaller FrankenTTS (0.6B) where the audio manifest says so. The recordings and weights are not in the game.
