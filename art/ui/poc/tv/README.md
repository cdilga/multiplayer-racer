# TV mocks (P1-U02)

Live layout mocks of the shared screen for the design review (G-DESIGN, Playtest-1 plan §3a). Static
files only, served from `art/ui/` (`node art/ui/lib/serve.mjs`, then open `/poc/tv/`); no CDN, no game
code. Tiles are live three.js: Spike J's Cruz Missile (vendored by `../vendor.mjs`) driving scripted laps
on a greybox outback loop. The HUD is one DOM layer positioned over the tile viewports, the way the game
would build it, so its cost is measured (`capture.mjs --perf`). Sizes come from `art/ui/tokens.json`
in TV px at 1080p, scaled by the output height, so 1080p, 4K, 21:9 and a phone host share one layout.

Every state opens from a URL fragment (`#grid&n=7&fp=2,5`); `#` is the contents page and **H** returns
to it. `states.js` lists them all; `capture.mjs` captures each at 1080p and 4K (grid states also at
21:9 and on a portrait phone) into `docs/evidence/P1-U02/`. The round-1 rework (P1-U02.2) has its own checks:
`grid-check.mjs` (CI: every tile the same area for N = 1..40, 64, 99 on six screens) and `capture-grid.mjs` (the
rendered page, captures, HUD pills, the Cooee flash) into `docs/evidence/P1-U02.2/`.

## Proposals (one each, with why; the owner redirects at P1-U04)

| Question | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Grid rule** (→ P1-R04; P1-U02.2, R95; round 5) | `grid.js` `layoutGrid`: try every row count R with C = ⌈N/R⌉ columns; the 1.2–2.0 aspect band scores each arrangement and the largest equal tile wins (ties: fewer empty cells); then the race grid **fills** the area (`fill`): every tile exactly the same size, no bands above, below or beside them | R95 (equal area); owner round 5: no gaps |
| **Gaps** (N = 3, 5, 7, 10, 13, 26, 27, 32…) | The C×R − N empty cells sit at the end of the last row, each exactly a tile, and also count as one run. Only they hold the join QR and the player list (the QR first); nothing else is ever taken from the tiles. The rest show the dotted paper backdrop | Owner round 5: the QR and positions never take space the layout wouldn't have wasted anyway |
| **Join QR during play** (round 5) | A full QR always says **Join now** (teal strip) with ROO7 and the address. With no spare cell that fits it, it docks in the footer (small QR, code, address); clicking it pauses and opens the pause menu's join card big (`paused&sub=join`). The pause menu always shows the join card beside it. With no spare cell for the list, every player's position docks in the footer (a ticker when they don't all fit, never cut) | Owner round 5 |
| **Per-tile HUD** (→ P1-R07) | Top-left: number badge + name (12 graphemes). Top-right: position and lap on **one line**. Bottom-left: boost meter, with autopilot/reconnecting chips above it. Wreck countdown mid-tile. Bottom-centre and bottom-right stay free. Every pill is one line high (1.5 em) | The top band is sky and the bottom-centre is your car in a chase view; the road ahead stays clear (master §12.1); one-line pills (POC1-06) |
| **HUD size** (P1-U02.2) | Text is 7.5% of the tile height, from 16 px (32 tiles at 1080p: a 29 px pill on a 210 px tile) to 32 px, × the screen scale. Below about 230 × 140 px the name goes; number, place and lap stay | The owner found the 32-tile HUD too large (POC1-06): it now scales down with the tile, below the TV text minimum of 24 px, which still holds for every other TV text |
| **Identify** (P1-U02.2) | "**Cooee #7**" over a transparent, high-exposure flash in the seat colour (the 3D under it brightened ×2.2 with a seat-colour wash): fast attack, 1.4 s decay, 1.5 s in all. The tile frame and badge pulse and the car is outlined in every tile. Reduced motion (P1-U05.6, POC2-15): no flash at all; the label shows for the same 1.5 s without scale and the frame holds. The mock replays it every 3 s | R99 (owner round 1, POC1-07); the flash sits under the HUD so the pills stay readable |
| **Cameras** (P1-U05.2, R98) | Third person by default, now higher and further back and aimed down the track, so the car is about half the share of the tile it was in round 0 and the road ahead shows. Camera distance is a setting: Near, Mid or Far, the host's default plus each player's override (`&dist=`, `&pdist=3:far`). **Segmented first person**: a bonnet cam pitched a touch down gives the road most of the tile, and a rear-view mirror takes the sky band. Every value is in `../shared/framing.json`, the TUNE input for P1-R05 | POC1-12: the old rig wasted the tile on scenery and a huge car |
| **Countdown** (P1-U05.2, R99) | One full-screen 3-2-1-GO with the Identify flash's high exposure (`countdown&n=8`). Cars hold until GO. Reduced motion has no flash and swaps the numbers (P1-U05.6) | POC1-13 |
| **Captions** (→ P1-A03; P1-U02.3; br-dim.8) | One component for every caption (`.capt`, `tokens.language.banner.caption`): the intermission subtitle's saffron brush strip, display italic caps, tag skew and tilt, two lines at most. Global only, never on one player's tile. Dynamic: centred in a spare cell when one is free after the QR and the standings. Otherwise, and always when static, flat on one line in the middle of the footer. Left to right: the join (small QR when no cell holds the big one, the room code, the address, the player count); the race readouts (race, lap, leader, clock) or a global caption; the logo; **Pause** (top-level); Fullscreen; the host menu. | **Pause flow** (P1-U02.3) | Anything that needs the whole screen pauses first. The pause card has a torn "Race paused" banner; "On this TV" (view, sound, reduced motion); "This room" (the dynamic/static layout, late joiners; laps shown, locked during a race); "Each player's own": camera and distance are per player (phones on the phone; pads and keyboards press Menu for settings over their own tile, `#padsettings`, their car on autopilot meanwhile); the join card beside it (round 5); Resume with burst ticks; Players and controllers, End round… and Disband room…. The footer stays, with Pause turned to Resume | POC1-09/10, in the language of `tv-pause-menu.jpg` (U01.3) |
| **Lobby** (br-dim.6; replaces the warm-up yard of P1-U02.4) | Nothing to drive: a generic, non-interactive crane view of the upcoming track, and the roster fills the screen so every player is visible at once at any N and aspect. One card per player: seat number, name, state (Joining…, Choosing car…, Ready). Density comes from the race grid's rule (`grid.js` `layoutGrid`, with a minimum tile size), run on the roster's box: the richest tier whose cards stay legible (number + name + state label, then number + name + state tick, then number + state tick, then number alone tinted by state), never a scroll, a page or a cap; a name is cut at 12 graphemes, as on the HUD. The join QR and Start race share the layout: a side column on landscape, a row above the roster and a button under it on portrait (`#lobby&n=1…150`, `&st=joining\|choosing\|ready` forces one state) | R110/br-dim.6: the lobby shows the room, not a yard; any N reads, never a "max players" |
| **Round complete + highlights** (br-dim.7; replaces the separate end-of-round and intermission screens) | One screen (`#results` and `#intermission` both open it). The highlight replay is the screen: 50 to 67 % of the screen area (`round-layout.json`, the rule as data: the video's share per aspect band, `stack` left or top), expanding vertically on the left under the heading on landscape, full width on top on portrait. Everything else is packed into the other region with no unused block over one button row: the placings (richer rows for the top three: trophy, points, seat colour; the podium cards are gone), the join QR and code, the next-race chip and the three host buttons. Everyone else is a grid of cells by `grid.js`'s rule (row, seat, number, then the tiniest), never a cap; the region's scale grows up to 1.5x when few players leave room (`#results&n=1…150`; `merged-check.mjs` measures it) | The owner's round-3 review: the highlights are the fun, so the video leads and the card/podium duplication goes; both old screens asked the same of the TV, so there is no reason to keep two |
| **Over-3D overlays** | On the per-player grid: an arrow at the tile edge points to your car when it's off-screen, and Identify outlines the car. Name plates over cars only on the Derby Overview, and other single-shared-screen modes | R100 (POC1-14): on the grid every player has their own tile and number |
| **Derby Overview** (design reference; Full; camera reworked in P1-U05.4) | One shared view of the bowl at a fixed 60° pitch, never rotating; identity rings under cars; thin edge bands for the timer and damage points; the host's Grid ↔ Overview switch. It frames each car now and 1.6 s ahead, follows on critically damped springs with speed limits and a soft dead zone, zooms out quicker than in, drifts at most 12 m from the bowl's centre and always holds the whole bowl (120 to 165 m of ground). `&cam=round0` shows round 0's per-frame refit for comparison | Master §6.4; R107 (POC2-06/07): round 0 made the owner motion sick |
| **Brush system** (round 4, br-awxz) | One shape family for every surface (`art/ui/sheets/brush-button.js`): buttons are brushed slabs (straight-ish edges, a forward lean, the ink outline following the slab, every state traced on the slab); badges are dabs, chips and HUD pills short slabs, cards and rows long slabs, list panels hand-cut sheets, all with the same outline and hard shadow and any fill. The plain button stays as the compact variant | Owner round 4: "a consistent brush adjacent style … straight-ish edges, commit to the design" |
| **Join QR, always labelled** (round 4) | A QR on screen always carries the room code and the address under it, on paper (no white box); with no room for both, the footer's join line carries them | Owner round 4: people must know it's a join link |

- Nameplate decluttering (Overview only now) is a simple nudge; P1-R07 owns the real rule.

- The 3D is a mock: scripted laps, no physics, cars may overlap.
